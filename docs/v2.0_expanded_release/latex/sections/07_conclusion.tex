\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

We introduced \textbf{X-CDS}, an explainable clinical decision support framework that mitigates LLM hallucinations. By combining hybrid retrieval (ChromaDB + BM25) with a stateful LangGraph self-correction loop, X-CDS programmatically verifies that diagnostic or therapeutic suggestions have verified, verifiable origins in medical literature. Ragas evaluation on a large clinical dataset confirms that X-CDS yields directional improvements in model faithfulness and robust context recall. Although these improvements were not statistically significant at the $\alpha=0.05$ level (Wilcoxon signed-rank test $p > 0.05$), they present a promising methodology to improve citation verification and mitigate hallucinations for LLMs in clinical decision support environments. Future research will explore clinician-in-the-loop evaluation with medical trainees to validate clinical safety in real-world hospital environments.
