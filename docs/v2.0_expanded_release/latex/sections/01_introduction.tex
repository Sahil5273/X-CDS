\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

Large language models can draft differential diagnoses, summarize guidelines, and suggest next steps in care. They can also state plausible recommendations that are not supported by the evidence in front of them. In clinical use, that gap between fluent text and verified fact is the main safety concern we address.

We built \textbf{X-CDS} (Explainable Clinical Decision Support) for emerging arbovirus management at VIT Bhopal: a retrieval pipeline that must attach each clinical claim to a retrieved WHO or PMC passage before the answer is released.

\subsection{The Arbovirus Differential Diagnosis Dilemma}
Our evaluation domain covers \textbf{Zika, Dengue, Chikungunya, and West Nile virus}. Fever, rash, and joint pain overlap across these infections, so early labels are often wrong. The cost of a wrong label is not symmetric:
\begin{itemize}
    \item A patient with severe joint pain may receive NSAIDs (e.g., ibuprofen or aspirin) when Chikungunya is suspected.
    \item If the true diagnosis is Dengue, NSAIDs can worsen platelet dysfunction and precipitate hemorrhagic complications.
    \item In pregnancy, missed Zika screening can delay detection of congenital Zika syndrome.
\end{itemize}

These cases motivated our design: retrieval must surface the right guideline language, and generation must not outrun what was retrieved. Standard retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) helps by adding literature to the prompt \citep{lewis2020retrieval}, but two failure modes remain common:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Retrieval omits the chunk that carries the critical fact.
    \item The generator paraphrases beyond what the retrieved text supports, or cites a source that does not contain the claim.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{X-CDS} targets both stages. Our contributions are:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item A hybrid retrieval stack (dense embeddings + BM25, RRF merge, cross-encoder re-ranking).
    \item A LangGraph guardrail loop that enforces minimum token overlap between cited sentences and source chunks.
    \item An empirical study on 100 arbovirus clinical scenarios, including threshold sweeps, pipeline telemetry, and qualitative case review on the pre-pilot evaluation corpus (6,940 chunks).
\end{enumerate}
